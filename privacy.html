Privacy Policy

How Positive At Work handles personal information submitted through the website.

Who we are

This website is operated by Positive At Work Limited, a UK company: www.positiveatwork.co.uk.

Cookies

Cookies are only used to enhance your experience of the website. We do not store personally identifiable information through cookies.

Embedded content from other websites

Articles may include embedded content such as videos, images or external articles. Embedded content behaves as if the visitor has visited the other website. Those websites may collect data, use cookies, embed third-party tracking and monitor interactions.

Who we share your data with

We do not share your details or data with external organisations unless requested by law enforcement organisations.

How long we retain your data

For visitors who send a message through the website, we store the personal information provided in that message and delete it once we have responded. If an ongoing business relationship begins, relevant interaction information may be stored in a separate CRM system. You can request to see this information at any time.

Your rights

You can request an exported file of the personal data we hold about you, including data you have provided. You can also request erasure of personal data we hold about you, excluding data we are required to keep for administrative, legal or security purposes.

Where your data is sent

We do not send your data anywhere else.
