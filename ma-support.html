M&A Support

Specialist transaction support for buyers and vendors, from acquisition research and due diligence to sale preparation, negotiation support and risk management.

For Buyers

Services for buyers

Specialised transaction and related services, including a variety of due diligence support services. Support can be provided at a fixed price or through a risk/reward agreement.

  • Commercial viability and investment reports.
  • Research, identification and qualification of suitable businesses to acquire.
  • Preparation of information requests, target liaison and results analysis.
  • Vendor activity management pre-offer, including Heads of Terms support.
  • Acquisition due diligence and buyer advisory during SPA negotiations.
  • Independent risk assessment and management.
  • Due diligence information and project management.
  • Integration planning and execution.
  • Operational due diligence across sales, marketing, software development, software service and management profiling.
For Vendors

Services for vendors

Support for founders and owners contemplating retirement sales, subsidiary disposals or other strategic exits. The work is intended to carry transaction pressure while the owner continues running the business.

  • Exit strategy advice to improve value, transaction speed and purchaser reliability.
  • Advice on exit routes, valuation models and owner workload.
  • High-quality Information Memorandum preparation.
  • Detailed buyer research and targeted purchaser approach.
  • Offer shaping, negotiation support and preferred purchaser selection.
  • Transaction team project management through completion.
Risk Management

Balanced risk control throughout due diligence.

Buyers usually seek low risk in the value they acquire. A live risk register helps ensure risks are understood, sized accurately and managed through completion, post-completion action plans, SPA protections or negotiation points.
