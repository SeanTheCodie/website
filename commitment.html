Commitment

Confidential, trusted and expert advice delivered with integrity and transparency.

Clients

  • We serve all clients with integrity, competence and objectivity.
  • We keep client information and engagement records confidential and use proprietary client information only with the client’s permission.
  • We do not use confidential client information for any purpose outside the agreed engagement.
  • Before undertaking an engagement, we disclose conflicts of interest that may compromise success.

Engagements

  • We only accept engagements for which we are qualified by experience and competence.
  • Staff and associates are assigned according to experience, knowledge and expertise, and only with express agreement.
  • We immediately acknowledge influences on objectivity and offer to withdraw where objectivity or integrity may be impaired.
  • We work to pre-agreed objectives that may flex as client needs change.
  • We agree targets, milestones, progress reviews and risk management approaches.

Fees

  • We agree the basis for fees and expenses in advance.
  • Fees and expenses are reasonable, legitimate and commensurate with the services delivered and responsibility accepted.
  • We disclose any expected fees or commissions for equipment, supplies or services recommended.

Professional conduct

  • We respect intellectual property rights of clients, consulting firms and colleagues.
  • We do not use proprietary information or methodologies without permission.
  • We do not advertise deceptively or misrepresent experience, competence or ability.
